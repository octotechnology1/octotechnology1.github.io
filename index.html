About the studio 🐙

OctoTechnology is a small independent studio. Like an octopus, we have many hands — and each one makes a new app for children.

We start every game by watching how children really play, and we only keep what a two-year-old can manage on their own. Every app is built for Android and iOS at the same time from one shared code base, so the iPhone and iPad versions grow together with the Android ones.

App series

Adventures of Mark the Cat 🐱

Our first series of apps has one hero. A ginger cat in round glasses is a kind grown-up friend for little ones: he never tests or scolds — he asks for help, shows the way when your child is unsure and celebrates every success. In each app of the series he has his own role.

Teacher

Reads stories and shows new words in TwoMeows

Mechanic

Builds and washes trucks in Mark's Garage

Host

Flies in on a balloon in Mark the Cat

Artist

Brings pictures to life in Mark's Art

Helper at home

Tidies up with your child in Mark's Home

“You can do it — Mark is right here.”

Made with care for parents 👨‍👩‍👧

These promises hold in every OctoTechnology app.

🌈

No way to lose

A wrong move just gently bounces back. No red, no error sounds, no timers.

🚫

No ads

No ads and no links out of the app — only play and learning.

🔒

Respect for privacy

Our games collect as little data as possible, and most collect none at all. Every policy is public.

👨‍👩‍👧

A parental gate

Settings and purchases sit behind a gate that little hands can't open.

✈️

Works offline

Play on the road, at the cottage or on a plane.

🗣️

Your language

The apps speak your child's language — up to 17 languages — and children don't need to read.

And for kids it's just play ⭐
