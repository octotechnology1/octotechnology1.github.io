Privacy Policy

Mark's Trains (the "Game") for iPhone and iPad is a railway game for children aged 2–5 made by Octo Technology Dev ("we"). This policy covers the iOS version of the Game distributed through the App Store. The Game is built so that it does not collect any personal data from children or from parents.

In short

No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no chat and no sharing with other players. The Game works offline. The only network request is the subscription check with the App Store, made by iOS itself.

What data we collect

None. The Game does not collect, store on any server or share with anyone any personal or other data, including name, age, e-mail, phone number, Apple Account details, device or advertising identifiers (the Game does not use the advertising identifier), location, contacts, photos, video, microphone audio, usage analytics or crash reports. We have no servers that the Game sends data to. In the App Store privacy details this is shown as "Data Not Collected".

Permissions

The Game does not ask for access to the camera, photos, microphone, location or contacts, and does not ask for permission to track.

What is stored on the device

Only the game progress (which levels are finished and which gifts were received for the depot shelf), the settings of the parents' section (sounds, voice, music, volume) and a mark that a subscription is active or that the unlock code has been entered. The language is taken from the device settings. All of it stays in the Game's own storage on the iPhone or iPad, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is deleted together with the Game. Like the data of any app, it may be included in a backup of the device that you make in iCloud or on a computer; such backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we have no access to them.

Videos and sounds

The short films about real trains, Mark's voice and the music are built into the Game. Nothing is streamed or downloaded while playing.

Subscriptions

The first three village levels (opened one after another) and the depot are free. The other levels, including new levels as they are added, can be opened with a monthly or yearly subscription. When a child taps a closed station, Mark asks them to call a grown-up and the parents' section opens behind a simple arithmetic question. Purchases are made only there and are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store under the Apple Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details, name, e-mail or any information about your Apple Account. To check whether a subscription is active, the Game asks the App Store on the device.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or with the "Manage in App Store" button in the parents' section. The "Restore purchases" button restores the subscription on another iPhone or iPad signed in with the same Apple Account. Use of the Game is governed by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.

Ads and third-party services

The Game has no ads of any kind and no tracking. Apart from Apple's StoreKit, which is part of iOS and is needed for subscriptions, it uses no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, attribution, sign-in, social features or data storage.

Links outside the Game

Only three links lead outside the Game — this policy, Apple's terms of use and the App Store subscriptions page — and all of them are inside the parents' section, behind the arithmetic question. There are no links, buttons or banners leading outside the Game in the children's part.

Children

The Game is designed for children aged 5 and under and is intended for the Kids category of the App Store. As Apple requires for kids apps, it has no ads, analytics or tracking, and purchases and links that lead outside the Game are available only in the parents' section behind the parental gate. Because no personal information is collected from children, the Game meets the requirements of the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation for children (GDPR, Article 8) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and no parental consent for data processing is required. There is no way for a child to share personal information in the Game, to talk to other people or to make a purchase without a grown-up.

Parents' rights

Because we do not hold any data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. Everything the Game keeps is on your device and can be removed at any time by deleting the Game. If you have a question about privacy, write to us — we answer every letter.

Security

Since no data leaves the device, there is no data that could leak from our side. Payments are protected by Apple.

Changes

If the Game ever starts to use any data, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated first, and the change will be described here with a new effective date.

Keďže sa osobné údaje detí nezbierajú, Hra spĺňa požiadavky amerického zákona o ochrane súkromia detí na internete (COPPA), všeobecného nariadenia EÚ o ochrane údajov v časti o deťoch (GDPR, článok 8) a britského kódexu pre detské služby (Age Appropriate Design Code); súhlas rodičov so spracovaním údajov nie je potrebný. Dieťa v Hre nemôže zdieľať osobné údaje, komunikovať s inými ľuďmi ani nič kúpiť bez dospelého.

Práva rodičov

Neuchovávame žiadne údaje o vás ani o vašom dieťati, takže nemáme čo ukázať, opraviť ani vymazať. Všetko, čo Hra uchováva, je vo vašom zariadení a môžete to kedykoľvek odstrániť spolu s Hrou. Ak máte otázku o ochrane súkromia, napíšte nám — odpovedáme na každý list.

Bezpečnosť

Údaje neopúšťajú zariadenie, takže z našej strany nemá čo uniknúť. Platby chráni Apple.
