About the app

Color with Mark the Cat! One tap fills an area with color, and when the picture is finished it comes alive: the train puffs and rolls, the balloon floats away, the rocket takes off, the fish swim.

Here Mark is an artist with a red beret, a brush and a palette. Made for children aged 2–5: big friendly buttons and no reading needed.
