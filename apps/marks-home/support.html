Purchases and subscriptions

Payments go through Apple or Google — we never see your card details. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time:

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions

Refunds

Refunds are handled by the store where you paid:

  • Apple — reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play — order history

For parents

Purchases and settings in our apps sit behind a parental gate, so children can't open them by accident.
